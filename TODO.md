Judging Criteria
Innovation & Creativity (15 points)
Is the idea original or a creative twist on an existing solution? Does it approach the problem in a unique or unexpected way? Is there a clear “wow” factor?
Technical Execution (15 points)
How well was the idea implemented? Is the technology used effectively and appropriately? Does the demo actually work? Are there bugs or unfinished features? Complexity: Did the team push technical boundaries?
Presentation & Demo (20 points)
Was the problem clearly explained? Did the team communicate their process and thought clearly? Was the demo smooth, well-organized, and within time limits?
